Partners for Baltic capital

We work with private investors and family offices from the Baltics. We also work with funds and institutions. They want to invest in German real estate. We help them do it.

Some look for a single property. Others want to build a portfolio. We know the German market and the people in it.

We are based in Vilnius and work across Germany.

How we help

  1. 01

    Access

    We know owners, agents, banks and developers across Germany. Some of the deals we see are never publicly listed.

  2. 02

    Judgement

    We know how German property is priced, financed, taxed and regulated. If a deal doesn't work, we say so.

  3. 03

    Execution

    We do the work on the ground: screening, due diligence, negotiation and closing.
